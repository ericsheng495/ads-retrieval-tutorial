Wide & Deep: combine memorization and generalization

Concept

Memorization means retaining the effect of a useful observed combination. Generalization means making useful predictions for combinations with little direct evidence. Neither word means “always good” or “always overfitting.”

Wide & Deep jointly trains a wide linear branch, which can include engineered cross features, and a deep branch using embeddings and nonlinear layers. Their contributions combine into the final prediction. The wide branch can retain specific patterns while the deep branch shares statistical strength. Cheng et al., 2016.

Architecture

The branches contribute to one logit before the sigmoid. Training them jointly does not mean averaging two separately calibrated probabilities.

Worked example

For Mira and the hiking ad, the cross hiking_interest AND hiking_category is 1. Suppose its wide coefficient contributes +0.7. The deep branch uses category, history, and numeric inputs and outputs +0.4. Add bias -2:

z = -2 + 0.7 + 0.4 = -0.9
p = sigmoid(-0.9) ≈ 0.289

For a new outdoor subcategory, the exact cross may have no reliable coefficient, while embeddings can share patterns with related examples. That possibility is why a generalizing branch is useful; it is not a guarantee of cold-start quality. An unseen category still needs a defined encoding or content path.

Implementation exercise

On a fixed toy interaction dataset, implement three variants: wide-only, deep-only, and the summed-logit joint model. Use the same training/validation split and loss. Start with one manually named cross so you can trace its effect. Then remove that cross and observe what the deep branch can learn by itself.

Measure log loss and calibration as well as AUC. Control embedding size and training budget; otherwise a larger model can appear better simply through more capacity. This is an architecture exercise, not a new feature of src/train.py.

Check your understanding

What useful information can a hand-engineered cross add beyond the separate feature indicators? Why might adding millions of crosses make training harder?
