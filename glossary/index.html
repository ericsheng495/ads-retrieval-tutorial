A plain-language recommender glossary

Term Meaning Example or caveat
User / query The person or request being served A session prefix can be the query without a known user ID.
Item / candidate Something the system could show A candidate is an item in a particular shortlist.
Catalog The collection of possible items Available inventory can change over time.
Context Information about the current situation Device, time, surface, recent history.
Feature An input available at prediction time Earlier interaction count; never the future outcome.
Label The target derived from an observed outcome Clicked or did not click under a stated exposure/window.
Impression A logged display opportunity Its precise meaning depends on instrumentation.
CTR Click-through rate: clicks / impressions A model may predict a per-impression click probability.
CVR Conversion rate under a stated denominator Per click and per impression are different quantities.
Eligibility Whether an item is allowed to be shown Budgets, targeting, policy, or inventory constraints.
Retrieval Finding a shortlist cheaply Search vectors or combine several candidate sources.
Ranking Scoring or ordering candidates Often uses a richer model than retrieval.
Re-ranking Adjusting an initial ordering Diversity, fatigue, or other product constraints.
Score A number used to compare candidates A similarity score need not be a probability.
Embedding A learned numerical representation An array of d numbers associated with an entity or features.
Tower / encoder A function that produces a representation Independent item computation enables cached item vectors.
Parameter A number learned during training A coefficient, bias, or embedding coordinate.
Hyperparameter A setting chosen outside the optimizer Learning rate, dimension, number of layers.
Logit Raw score before a sigmoid or softmax It can be negative or larger than one.
Sigmoid A function mapping a logit into (0,1) Produces a binary-event probability parameter.
Softmax A function turning candidate logits into a distribution Candidate probabilities sum to one within that set.
Loss A quantity measuring training disagreement Reducing it does not guarantee online improvement.
Gradient How a small parameter change affects the loss The optimizer uses it to choose an update direction.
Batch A group of examples processed together Other batch items can provide retrieval alternatives.
Epoch One pass through the training examples More passes can overfit.
Negative A contrasting or nonpositive example An unobserved item is not necessarily disliked.
Feature interaction / cross A joint effect beyond individual feature effects Topic interest together with matching ad category.
Sparse feature A representation with mostly zero entries A one-hot category has one active position.
Dense feature A representation with many usable numerical entries A learned embedding or normalized numeric measurements.
MLP / DNN A stack of learned layers and nonlinear transformations A flexible function from features to scores or vectors.
Multi-task learning Learning several predictions in one system Click and purchase tasks may share some representations.
Expert / gate A learned subnetwork / a learned mixing function A gate weights expert outputs for an input.
Offline evaluation Testing on a fixed logged dataset Its candidate and label assumptions matter.
Online experiment Comparing actual outcomes under deployed policies Interference may couple treatment and control.
Calibration Agreement between predicted probabilities and frequencies Correct ordering alone does not establish calibration.
Leakage Inputs contain information unavailable at prediction time A future conversion accidentally enters a feature.
Selection bias Observations reflect an earlier selection process Only auction winners can produce displayed-ad feedback.
Interference One experimental unit's treatment affects another's outcome Treatment spends a budget shared with control.
ANN Approximate nearest-neighbor search Trades exact search work for possible missed neighbors.
Cold start Making predictions with little or no past interaction data A new ad needs a content/fallback path.
p95 latency A time at or below which 95% of measured requests finish State workload, batching, and hardware.
Ablation Removing or changing one component for comparison Compare a deep branch with and without a cross branch.
